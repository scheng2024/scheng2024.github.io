## Research Internship + Potential PhD (2027) — QWERTY

I am recruiting a Master's intern to develop a reproducible,
machine-learning-ready data pipeline for historical wildfires in France
and Europe. The project will support wildfire simulation, prediction and
parameter calibration using FlamMap and PyTorchFire.

This research is part of the PEPR FORESTT QWERTY project and is a
collaboration between CEREA (ENPC, Institut Polytechnique de Paris)
and EPHE. The internship will be jointly supervised by Sibo Cheng
and Christelle Hély.

A potential PhD continuation will focus on mapping wildfire burn
probabilities across France using calibrated simulation ensembles and
learning region-specific fire parameters from biomass, vegetation and
climate data. Continuation to the PhD will depend on internship performance
and mutual agreement.

[Read the internship and potential PhD announcement (PDF)]({{ '/stage_qwerty_2027.docx.pdf' | relative_url }})
