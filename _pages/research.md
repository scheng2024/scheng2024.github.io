My research combines data assimilation, scientific machine learning and
reduced-order modelling to reconstruct, predict and control complex
physical systems from limited observations.

My main interests include latent-space data assimilation, physics-aware
representation learning, generative models and differentiable simulation,
with applications to wildfire, atmospheric science and fluid dynamics.

## Research Projects

### Hi! PARIS Starting Fellowship

**Differentiable Digital Twin for Wildfire Prediction & Control**

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Programme:** Hi! PARIS Internal Chairs / Fellowships, 2026 cohort

This project develops a differentiable digital twin for wildfire
prediction and control, connecting physical modelling with machine
learning. The research focuses on differentiable wildfire simulation,
calibration and prediction to support effective fire management.

[Official project announcement](https://hi-paris.fr/2026/04/announcing-the-2026-hi-paris-chairs/)

### OFML — Machine Learning for Wildfire Fighting Strategies

**Optimizing Real-Time Wildfire Fighting Strategies Using Machine Learning Techniques**

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Funding:** ANR — ANR-25-CE56-0198  
**Period:** 2025–2029

OFML develops efficient wildfire prediction models that account for
human interventions, including firebreaks and retardant deployment.
It combines GPU-based simulation, deep learning and reinforcement
learning to optimise firefighting decisions, with a focus on France
and the Mediterranean region. I coordinate the project and lead its
research on efficient wildfire modelling and optimisation of
firefighting strategies.

[Official ANR project page](https://anr.fr/Project-ANR-25-CE56-0198)

### MilaDy — Machine Learning for Turbulent Dynamos

**Role:** Co-Investigator  
**Project Coordinator:** [Caroline Nore](https://perso.limsi.fr/nore/), LISN, Université Paris-Saclay  
**Funding:** ANR — ANR-25-CE51-0077  
**Start:** 2026

MilaDy combines machine learning and numerical simulation to study
turbulent magnetohydrodynamic dynamos: conducting fluid flows that
generate magnetic fields. The project investigates magnetic-field
reversals, rare dynamical events and the influence of physical
parameters.

At CEREA/ENPC, our research focuses on physics-aware representation
learning and reduced-order modelling of three-dimensional velocity
and magnetic fields, connecting learned representations to data
assimilation.

[Official ANR project page](https://anr.fr/Projet-ANR-25-CE51-0077)

### QWERTY — Forest Ecological Vulnerability to Wildfires

**Quantifying the Vulnerability of Forest Ecological Values to Wildfires**

**Role:** Co-Investigator  
**Project Coordinator:** [Florent Mouillot](https://www.cefe.cnrs.fr/fr/recherche/ef/forecast/829-c/137-florent-mouillot), IRD  
**Funding:** France 2030 — PEPR FORESTT, ANR-25-PEFO-0008  
**Period:** 2025–2029

QWERTY assesses wildfire impacts beyond burned area and biomass loss.
It combines ecological observations, satellite data and modelling to
map forest ecological values, evaluate vulnerability and post-fire
recovery, and support ecological risk assessment.

At CEREA/ENPC, we develop GPU-accelerated wildfire modelling with
PyTorchFire, supporting the assessment of combustion and
greenhouse-gas emissions within WP3.

[Official project page](https://www.pepr-forestt.com/eng/projects/qwerty)

[Project presentation](https://www.pepr-forestt.fr/content/download/785/7258?version=1)
